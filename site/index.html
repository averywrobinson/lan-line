Why Lan-Line

Two computers shouldn’t feel like two planets.

Your Mac and Windows machine sit twelve inches apart. Moving a screenshot, config snippet, or asset shouldn’t require a cloud server, an account, or a workaround.

Slack / Discord self-DMs

Zero accounts. Zero compression.

Stop cluttering work chat with screenshots and keys. Files arrive full-resolution over your local network.

AirDrop / Universal Clipboard

Built for Mac ↔ Windows

No Apple-only walls and no silent clipboard overwrites. Keep a clean, visible drop stream across OSes.

Cloud Drives

Instant, not synced

No upload queues or leftover junk files clogging Drive or iCloud. Drop it, grab it, move on.

SMB Network Shares

Text, images & files in one spot

Skip mounting network volumes just to pass a URL, SSH snippet, or 2 KB screenshot across the desk.

Crafted for Daily Flow

Small details that make it stick.

Designed to stay quiet until the second you need it—and get out of your way immediately after.

Always Pinned

Toggle the pushpin to float Lan-Line cleanly above your active apps—always one drag away, never buried under thirty tabs.

Paste Screenshots Directly

Hit Cmd+V or Ctrl+V with a raw image on your clipboard. Lan-Line auto-wraps it into a clean, timestamped PNG chip ready to drag out.

One-Click Copy & Delivery Checks

Every text bubble and file chip ships with an inline copy button and instant delivery checkmark—no highlighting, no guessing.

Wakes on Receive

Keep Lan-Line minimized while you work. The moment your other machine sends a file or link, the window pops up ready for you.

Direct Local LAN

Machine to machine over your local network. Zero cloud relays, zero logins, no size cap beyond your free disk space.

Live Status at a Glance

Emerald green means linked and ready; slate gray means standing by for your peer. Zero nested preference menus to babysit.

30-Second Setup

Open on both screens. Start dropping.

01 / CONNECT

Launch on Mac & Windows

Open Lan-Line on both computers. One shows a short pairing code; pick it on the other and type the code. No accounts, no cloud sign-in.

02 / DROP

Drag, paste, or type

Throw in a file, paste a clipboard screenshot, or send a link or terminal snippet. Works identically in both directions.

03 / GRAB

Copy or drag straight out

The receiving window wakes up instantly. Click copy on text or drag the file chip straight into Finder, Explorer, or any open app.

Get Lan-Line.

A normal installer for each computer. Install it on both, pair once with a short code, and start dropping.

  • macOS 14 or later (Apple Silicon) · Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
  • 100% local LAN transfers — no accounts, no telemetry
  • Files, text snippets & pasted PNGs — as big as your disk allows
